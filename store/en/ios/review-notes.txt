Dice Den is an offline Yatzy dice game. There is no login and no account.

Ads
- Interstitial ads appear only after the player leaves the final scorecard (Home or Play again). They never appear during a game, on app open, on the pass-the-phone screen or in Today's dice.
- None in the first session or before 3 completed games; then at most 1 per 2 completed games and 1 per 4 minutes. All ads are muted.
- Rewarded ads are optional and opt-in: "Try for 24 h" on a dice skin or table in the Shop, and "Replay today's dice" after today's game is finished. At most 3 a day. They never give extra rolls or help with the score.
- Consent: Google's UMP form in the EEA and UK before any ad request. The App Tracking Transparency prompt appears after the 3rd session, on the Home screen, never during a game, after a short explanation with a single Continue button.

In-app purchases (all non-consumable, one-time)
- Remove Ads: removes the interstitials. Rewarded ads stay available because they are opt-in.
- Classic Dice Pack, Table Pack, Complete Collection: cosmetic dice skins and tables only. They do not change the dice, which use one fair random generator for every player.
- Supporter Bundle: Remove Ads plus every current and future skin and table.
- Restore Purchases is in the Shop and in Settings. Prices come from the App Store.

To see an interstitial quickly: finish 3 games, close and reopen the app, finish another game and tap Home.
