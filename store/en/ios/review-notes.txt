Dice Den is an offline Yatzy dice game. There is no login, no account and no network feature.

- Nothing to sign in to, nothing to buy and nothing to watch. This version has no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking, so there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
- The dice use the device's secure random number generator for every player, including the computer players. The Stats screen shows every face rolled so the fairness can be checked.
- Games, scores and settings stay on the device. Settings and progress sync only through the player's own iCloud key-value storage, if iCloud is available.
- Notifications are optional: Settings, then Daily reminder asks for permission only when the player turns it on.

To see everything quickly
1. Open the app and tap Skip on the tutorial (or Show me how).
2. Play, then Start game. Tap Roll, tap dice to hold them, tap a score box to fill it. Thirteen turns finish a Classic game.
3. Today's dice on Home: the same rolls for everyone on a given day.
4. Pass and play (up to 6 people) and vs Computer (Easy, Normal, Expert) are on the New game screen.
5. Stats and Settings are on Home.
