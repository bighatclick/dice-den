Dice Den is the classic Yatzy dice game, made calm again. Roll five dice up to three times a turn, hold the ones you want, and fill the score pad box by box. It plays just like the paper score pad you grew up with.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Solo: chase your high score.
• Against the computer: Easy, Normal or Expert. The computer rolls the same dice you do and never sees what comes next.
• Pass and play: up to 6 people around one phone, each with a name and colour. A handoff screen hides the last player's dice.
• Today's dice: everyone gets the same rolls on the same day. Keep a daily streak and share your score.

FAIR DICE YOU CAN CHECK
Every face has an equal 1 in 6 chance, from your phone's secure random number generator. Nobody gets lucky rolls for being behind, and the dice never change with the difficulty. Stats shows every face you and the computer have rolled, so you can see it for yourself.

MADE FOR EVERY TABLE
• Classic rules with the 13-box score pad, upper bonus, extra Yatzys and joker rule.
• Scandinavian Yatzy with 15 boxes, one tap away.
• Score hints you can turn off, and undo for your last score.
• Big dice, high-contrast pips, dark mode, left-hand layout and Reduce Motion.
• Your game is saved after every roll. Close the app any time and pick up where you left off.
• Plays offline. No account, no sign-in.

Dice Den is a dice game for family and friends, for a quiet coffee break or a lively game night. Roll the dice and fill the pad.
