Dice Den support

Dice Den is an offline Yatzy dice game for one player, against the computer, or passed around a table.

Common questions

My game disappeared. Games are saved after every roll. Open Dice Den and tap Resume game on Home.

How do I check the dice are fair? Open Stats and scroll to Your dice. It counts every face you and the computer have rolled.

How do I turn on the daily reminder? Settings, then Daily reminder. iOS asks for permission the first time.

How do I see the tutorial again? Settings, then Show the tutorial again.
