Dice Den privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Dice Den has no account, no login, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send your games, scores or settings to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your games, scores, statistics and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is available, your settings and progress are also kept in your own iCloud key-value storage so they follow you between your devices. The developer cannot read that storage.

Permissions

The app asks for permission only to send a daily reminder notification, and only if you turn that reminder on in Settings. You can turn it off at any time. It does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Fair dice

The dice use your device's secure random number generator for every player. Nothing is sent over the network to make a roll.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if advertising is added, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated before the new version is released.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/bighatclick/dice-den/issues.
